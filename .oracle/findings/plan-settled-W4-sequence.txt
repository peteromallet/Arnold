## SEQ4-1 — Pre-spawn exception semantics are internally contradictory

**Materiality:** High; affects criteria 4, 7, 9, and crash safety.

**Evidence:** §4.8 says `released_no_launch` creates no terminal worker fingerprint and permits a new reservation. §4.11 and NBF-02 acceptance say an exception before `mark_final_launch_entered()` becomes an `ordinary_terminal_failure` after canonical outcome recording. §4.12 says ordinary terminal failures reach existing consumers, while §4.10 forbids scheduling conditions from entering failure/breaker accounting.

The plan does not define whether a pre-spawn exception:
- records a terminal fingerprint;
- increments failure/breaker counters;
- may be retried identically;
- is merely reconciled as no-launch.

Those outcomes are materially different.

**Smallest correction:** Define a distinct typed `no_launch` outcome, or explicitly specify that pre-spawn normalization is excluded from terminal-failure projection, fingerprint reservation, and breaker accounting. Define ordering: reconciliation first, then consumer projection. Add restart and identical-redispatch tests.

**Criterion impact:** Final-launch exceptions, fingerprint blocking, scheduling/failure separation.

**North Star disposition:** Supports “deaths speak” and prevents an unlaunched worker from producing contradictory death/failure state.
