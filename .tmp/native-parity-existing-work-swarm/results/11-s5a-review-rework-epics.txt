### 4. What Still Must Be Built

1. **Source-visible rework loop** in `.pypeline` — the `review → execute(rework) → review` cycle must be a visible loop construct, not a handler-owned `"rework"` route_signal with `next_step="execute"` side effect (`review.py:1077-1082`)

2. **Source-visible rework cap** — `max_review_rework_cycles` must be declared on a source construct (e.g., `LoopPolicy`), not read from `get_effective("execution", cap_key)` in handler (`review.py:1033`)

3. **Source-visible pass/rework/blocked/force_proceeded route decisions** — `_resolve_review_outcome`'s decision tree must be moved into a declared policy or pure phase body attached to named source constructs; the checker must reject handler-owned route decisions

4. **Policy-as-route-table rejection for S5** — `components.py:2664-2669` `reducer_routes` are route tables with `target_ref`; the checker currently passes on Mapping existence of `REVIEW_POLICY.force_proceed_authority` without rejecting the route table (`master-plan.md:190, 856`)

5. **Split-outcome scenarios** for: review cap with blockers, review cap without blockers (advisory-only → force_proceeded), rework loop, no-review terminal, deferred-human terminal, finalize fallback revise (`master-plan.md:742`)

6. **Per-sprint dead-delete mutation** — mutate/delete old review/finalize route surfaces in `components.py` and prove behavior unchanged against frozen S1a baselines (`master-plan.md:743`)

---

### 5. Recommended Adjustment to Sprint Plan

**S5 should be split into S5a (Review + Rework) and S5b (Finalize + Final Projection)** — this mirrors how S1 was split. The current scope lumps review fanout/fanin/reducer/rework-cap/force-proceed with finalize artifact/fallback/projection, but these have different carrier sets.

**S5a scope:** extract only the review fanout/fanin → reducer → rework-loop-with-cap → pass/rework/blocked/force_proceeded. The checker must reject `_resolve_review_outcome` as route authority and require source-visible constructs. Delete handler-owned route signals and `components.py:2664-2669` `reducer_routes` as route tables.

**S5b scope:** finalize artifact/fallback/final-projection routes. These are simpler but have their own `FINALIZE_POLICY` surface in `components.py:1405-1508`.

**Pre-extraction prerequisite:** The S5a checker must be hardened in S1a/S1b to **reject** Mapping-existence checks on `REVIEW_POLICY.cap_thresholds` and `force_proceed_authority` as row authority before S5a extraction begins. Currently `_implemented_s5_boundary_rows` at `source_compiler.py:1821-1834` passes those rows on `isinstance(..., Mapping)` checks — this gate must fail open (produce diagnostics) when only policy-surface existence is available without source-visible rework construct.
