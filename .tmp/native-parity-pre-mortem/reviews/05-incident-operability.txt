## Verdict

**One composed history does not yet let a human explain and safely repair the run without hidden handler state.** The gaps are:

1. **Missing diagnostics** (gaps 1, 2, 5, 8): The causal chain from source construct → handler decision → state transition is not reconstructable from persisted artifacts. An operator sees `state: blocked` but cannot determine whether that was a `workflow.pypeline` branch, a handler policy decision, or a watchdog-issued blocker classification.

2. **Missing causal joins** (gaps 3, 9, 10): Suspension, resume, and human decisions lack durable identity linking the decision to the consumed transition. Drift is invisible.

3. **Missing quarantine states** (gaps 3, 4, 7, 9): There is no plan-level indicator for "suspended under old topology," "partial crash recovery," "stale repair projection," or "resumed under migration." The system proceeds silently through states that a human operator needs to see.
