### III. Summary

**11 infrastructure components exist** across generated ledgers, scenario hashes, installed-package fingerprints, handler-purity scans, dead-delete mutation checks, compatibility quarantine checks, the evidence-pack verifier, the conformance suite, and the strict checker framework.

**Of eight required corrections:** 0 are fully mechanically enforced. 5 are partial (strict checker has a bypass, dead-delete is monolithic, compat quarantine is evidence-only, generated-only conformance lacks AST enforcement, split-outcome coverage doesn't cross-check row coverage). 3 are missing entirely (current-shape negative fixture, policy-as-route-table rejection, runtime narrowing documentation).
