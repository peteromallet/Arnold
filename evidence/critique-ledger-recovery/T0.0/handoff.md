manifest was available in the live local evidence.

`RA-CONTAIN` append-only owner interface in an isolated worktree, with exact
tuple binding, grant/fence/CAS semantics, deny-all-effects/read-preserving
policy, TTL/termination, revoke/audit query, and independent verification.

start T0.1.
