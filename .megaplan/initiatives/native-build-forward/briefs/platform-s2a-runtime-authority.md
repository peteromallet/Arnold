# Platform S2A — Product-neutral runtime, admission, and authority

## Outcome

Generalize proven runtime lifecycle and completion semantics in place without changing Native meaning or enabling a second kernel.

## Scope

Extract resolution, lifecycle, admission, authority, isolation, completion binding/evaluation, attempts/sessions, continuation, repair dispositions, and failure semantics through typed transition.

## Generalize only proven durability

After Native S6 proves the control-plane/autonomy path, fold its reusable
operator simplifications here: one lifecycle/receipt authority, event-driven
supervision, typed pause/hold/replay/recovery, canonical liveness, and
source/object/package custody. Generalize R17 and R20 plus the duplicate
reader/writer lessons from R03A/R14–R19 without creating a second kernel,
ledger, planner, or monitoring ceremony. Product-specific Native semantics stay
in the Native chain. The accepted boundaries are recorded in the [compact
launch/durability root map](../handoff/launch-and-durability-root-map-20260903.md).
