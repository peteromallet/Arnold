# S7 - Native-Topology Conformance on the M11 Proof Framework

## Objective

Extend—not duplicate—the admitted Custody M11 conformance framework with
Native-specific topology and identity proof. Close only when clean checkout,
wheel/sdist, and pinned cloud runtime demonstrate the same authored semantics,
authority decisions, custody ownership, WBC history, and disposable projections.

`../GOLDEN_TRACE_CONTRACT.md` is the human-reviewed required proof-map input.
An independent static source oracle and separately implemented raw-event audit
verifier check Python/runtime equivalence. It is never runtime route or repair
authority.

## Semantic gate

- Source/lowering/runtime topology and dynamic child sets are equal; every
  route, loop, fanout/reducer, suspension/reentry, call-site policy, and
  terminal outcome is source-determined.
- Authored decision occurrences, accepted Run Authority decision IDs/outcomes/
  CAS sequences, and consumed runtime transitions/actions are exactly equal.
- Installed and cloud execution match checkout topology and split-outcome
  behavior.
- Zero hidden product-route authority remains in components, handlers,
  runtime maps, `_core`, compatibility/native-program projections, auto, or CLI.
- All golden families preserve same-run order, partial-order sibling freedom,
  occurrence multiplicity, exact joins, effects, and one terminal across all
  three install forms.
- All six extension mutations pass at the intended edit point and fail from
  handler/auto/metadata-only bypasses; the one-topology/readability contract
  has a reviewed acceptance receipt.
- Determinism, diagnostics/source maps, local-harness equivalence, LLM/tool
  replay/budgets/cache, checkpoint payloads, pin retention, namespace isolation,
  and contract-stack non-authority all have blocking accepted receipts.
- The five execution modes and enforcement dispositions pass `NP-DX-001`
  through `NP-DX-004`; edited-step iteration and explicit forks are low-friction
  while every unsafe promotion, identity reuse and silent changed-code resume
  remains blocked.
- Addressed exits, canonical decisions, frozen/keyed fanout, declared errors,
  typed reconfigure, agentic protocol, product-contract pinning, comparison
  provenance, all-plane writer proof, scheduler/repair boundaries, concrete
  legacy-table inertness, and all three measurable ergonomics gates are green.
- Production-store CAS provenance/contention, governed registry evolution,
  manifest mixed-worker compatibility, receipt incarnation/high-water binding,
  comparison-token forgery and volatile-arbitration-field mutations are green.

## Custody-adoption gate

- Every authoritative runtime action is covered by the admitted validator in
  enforce mode with current independent fence and epoch, exact target, and
  required WBC boundary evidence.
- The final proof consumes the pinned M11 conformance receipt and proof map;
  neither is copied, reimplemented, or replaced by Native-only status.
- The final validator demonstrably consumed `final-proof-map.json`; existence
  and hashing without semantic consumption cannot pass.
- Projection/evidence deletion, rebuild, forgery, and replay cannot increase
  authority; WBC remains durable history, never a grant or lease.

## Do not close if

- The final report says implemented while any blocking check is red.
- The proof generator begins from rows already labeled implemented.
- A clean installed end-to-end workflow has not actually executed.
- The validator did not consume the declared proof map or its pre-receipt hash
  is absent from the validation receipt.
- The refreshed old self-declared/hash-only ledger passes.
- The golden oracle or composed explanation is used to choose a runtime route,
  or any golden trace is stitched from different runs.
- An ordinary extension requires a handler/auto/metadata route addition or
  multiple manually synchronized identity/control-plane registries.
- Ambient nondeterminism, a forged cache, invalid checkpoint reference, missing
  pinned executable, or namespace collision can pass or silently recompute.
- CAS proof relies on serialized/local execution, a receipt can cross store
  incarnations or omit its history cursor, or registry/manifest evolution can
  admit an unsupported worker or comparison producer.
- A comparison record becomes admitted, an unregistered plane executes, a
  product-contract edit waives evidence, or any measurable ergonomics gate is
  advisory/red.
- Any runner infers or silently changes mode, fresh experiment identity requires
  production migration ceremony, or preview/sandbox/comparison can produce an
  admitted/certification claim or reuse production effect/history identity.
