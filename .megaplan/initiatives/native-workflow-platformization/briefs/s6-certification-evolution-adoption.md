# S6 — Certification, evolution, and adoption

Rerun the mechanical completion-crosswalk validator and require exact equality
across every requirement/constraint ID, canonical owner milestone, proof rule,
and accepted proof artifact. Consume the final historical-evidence disposition
ledger and prove every required missing/drifted `native-platform-followup`
artifact was recovered or explicitly re-proved before public certification.

## Objective

Incorporate the S5 adversarial findings, freeze only the versions whose complete
capability closure is proved, and publish the stable component standard,
packages, registry governance, evolution/DX contract and content-addressed
completion evidence. Leave every unproven product abstraction or compiler
internal explicitly experimental.

## Normative contract and inputs

[`../PLATFORM_CONTRACT.md`](../PLATFORM_CONTRACT.md) is the normative stable-
certification target after the explicit S5 amendments are incorporated. Consume
the Native Parity completion and handoff manifests plus the complete S1–S5
content-addressed handoff chain, cumulative proof map, inherited golden/DX
history, second-consumer challenge report and separate compatibility receipts.
No earlier experimental pin or registry label is itself publication evidence.

Completion schemas, including the persisted binding envelope, are internally
versioned from Native C1/M1. Native S2R GO-0 remains the start of authoritative
persisted-wire compatibility and decoder enforcement. S6 is the first and only
milestone that may publish a stable public completion authoring/API contract;
publication does not create a second runtime enablement or rewrite inherited
wire history.

The final chain validator consumes:

- `scripts/validate_native_workflow_platform_conformance.py`;
- `docs/arnold/native-workflow-platform-conformance.yaml`;
- `docs/arnold/native-workflow-platform-traceability.yaml`; and
- `.megaplan/initiatives/native-workflow-platformization/final-proof-map.json`.

## Receipt-bound stable publication

All candidate package/descriptor/profile/registry changes remain experimental
through merge. The final gate runs against merge HEAD and emits the accepted
S6 receipt. Only an explicit publication transaction that validates and
consumes that exact receipt may promote named versions to `stable`, publish
their stable conformance manifests, or activate stable registry discovery. The
transaction is atomic and binds the merged tree, final proof-map hash,
component/graph-lock digests, registry/proof-store incarnations, applicable
profiles, and exact version set. Wrong-tree, pre-merge, stale, red, partial,
cross-incarnation, copied-verdict, and replayed-receipt attempts leave every
candidate experimental.
The publication operation runs only as the chain's declared S6 transition
after final-readiness validation. It emits one immutable publication receipt;
the separate post-transition final validator consumes that receipt and
recomputes the stable state before S6 or the epic can complete.

## Locked decisions

- Only S6 may promote descriptor/profile/package/pattern versions from
  experimental to stable, and only after the unrelated S5 consumer and all
  mechanically applicable conformance profiles pass.
- Stable certification covers all eleven closure clauses and all 38 acceptance-
  suite families in the platform contract. Irrelevant profiles may be omitted
  only because effective capability closure mechanically excludes them.
- Preview, sandbox and comparison histories cannot be relabeled as production
  or certification evidence. Promotion creates or consumes exact pinned
  admission/certification records.
- Compatibility/change classes remain distinct for Python API, descriptor,
  source topology/body, generated manifest/producer registration, prompts/
  models/tools/policy, durable state/checkpoints, dependencies, effects and
  observable traces. New-instance and resume compatibility remain separate.
- Stable proof binds production CAS/store/adapter provenance, proof-registry and
  producer-registry incarnation/high-water, manifest schema/hash, root accepting
  actor/arbitration provenance, exact trace field contract and raw export.
- Registry states are `experimental`, `stable`, `deprecated`, and `withdrawn`,
  with evidence-backed transitions, migration/deprecation windows, retained
  artifact promises and safe GC.
- `withdrawn` is the canonical Platform term for the old completion proposal's
  `tombstoned` API state: the version remains historically addressable and
  non-reusable, cannot be newly selected or admitted, and retains the promised
  evidence/migration metadata. This is a terminology mapping, not a weaker
  lifecycle state.
- Product variability stays in typed bindings. Unproven abstractions, product
  semantics and internal compiler APIs do not become stable by proximity.
- The one machine `docs/arnold/workflow-execution-mode-dispositions.yaml`
  registry is normative;
  prose, CLI, editor, and docs tables remain generated/informative.
- The completion candidate-outcome registry is separately canonical and
  versioned. S6 publishes both registries and their generated total,
  version-bound boundary mapping without collapsing their types.
- Trace-field/volatile-allowlist changes require independent governance,
  invalidate affected receipts, and replay pinned raw histories. A new version
  cannot retroactively reinterpret failure.

## Gates

### Semantic gate

- The final platform satisfies all eleven contract clauses simultaneously; the
  two clean-installed consumers use the same implementations while retaining
  different typed product meanings, policies, effects, storage and supported
  shapes.
- `.pype` source is sole route authority; manifests, registries, handlers,
  schedulers, adapters, projections, CLI/auto and evidence cannot add or erase
  routes or authorize work.
- Exactly-one files, canonical child imports, package-owned optional default
  and explicit selection, private-step isolation, shared `.py` steps, aliases,
  cycles/recursion/collisions, physical moves, logical migrations, preview-only
  `.py` workflows, and installed resolution pass their positive and negative
  fixtures without source execution or declaration-order meaning.
- Resume/replay does not repeat accepted human, LLM or external work; edited
  experiments remain easy and isolated; stable executions retain exact
  RA/Custody/WBC, production CAS, effect and executable truth.

### Proof gate

- The final validator proves all mechanically applicable profiles and all 38
  acceptance families from raw, content-addressed evidence. Missing/red/fake-
  only/self-certified/unconsumed rows, unknown trace fields, partial maps/policy,
  resource-ledger violations, missed DX thresholds or hash-only proof fail.
- Every validator self-mutation fails for its named reason; a validator that
  accepts red/stale/unbound/unconsumed/extra/missing/self-certified/
  cross-incarnation/stitched evidence cannot certify.
- Checkout, clean wheel and cloud select the same locked graph and partial-order
  history. Both consumers pass the same black-box conformance contract and
  product-neutral explanation.
- Separate new-instance and resume-compatible receipts, one admitted migration,
  pinned-old support and breaking-change quarantine are all green.
- Stable manifests bind exact production store/service linearizability and
  adapter provenance, registry incarnation/high-water, generated-manifest
  schema/hash, root result/arbitration/accepting actor, and independent raw-
  before-normalized verification.

### Adoption gate

- Only proved versions enter `stable`; unsupported capabilities or abstractions
  remain `experimental`, and deprecated/withdrawn transitions enforce the
  published support and retention contract.
- Stable state and discovery change only through the exact post-merge S6
  receipt-consuming transaction; final validation alone does not publish.
- Public docs and local tooling allow a third consumer to compose, test,
  install, observe and evolve components using only documented surfaces.
- The reusable-pattern registry and content-addressed completion manifest are
  accepted by the final validator and proof-map consumer.

## Do not close this sprint if

- any of the eleven closure clauses or 38 acceptance families lacks applicable,
  independently consumed evidence;
- stable status precedes S5 challenge or mechanically derived capability proof;
- any stable publication occurs before exact S6 receipt consumption or can
  partially publish the version set;
- a preview/comparison result, fake CAS, normalized-only trace, producer self-
  check, projection, stitched history or hash without semantic consumption is
  counted as certification;
- either consumer imports/copies the other, requires hidden APIs, or receives
  different shared semantics;
- the unrelated-consumer independence scan is missing or cannot distinguish
  allowed generic platform imports from forbidden Megaplan coupling;
- the production profile deriver, trace allowlist owner, or final validator is
  its own sole effective oracle;
- active pinned runs can lose required code/prompt/tool/state/effect evidence
  before migration/quarantine/retention obligations end; or
- the final validator, traceability matrix, proof map, registry or completion
  manifest is incomplete, red or internally inconsistent.

## Non-goals

- Certifying unproven Megaplan-specific abstractions or compiler internals.
- Building a workflow marketplace, rebuilding M11/Native substrate, supporting
  arbitrary durable Python/open event streams, or standardizing product values.
- Treating epic preparation, documentation, or a green narrative review as
  completion evidence.
