# S5 — Adversarial second consumer and substitutability

## Objective

Challenge—not merely demonstrate—the candidate platform with a genuinely
unrelated workflow and an independent compatible implementation. Force the
standard through different domain semantics, bindings and composition shapes;
separately prove new-instance and suspended-run compatibility; and record every
abstraction retained, revised, removed or left experimental before S6 freezes
anything stable.

## Normative contract and inputs

[`../decisions/PLATFORM_CONTRACT.md`](../decisions/PLATFORM_CONTRACT.md) remains normative, but its
candidate version is intentionally revisable in this sprint through explicit,
versioned amendments and migrated receipts. Consume the exact Native Parity
handoff, the S1 candidate contract/corpus, S2A runtime, S2B authoring core,
S3 developer tooling, and S4 content-addressed extraction/recomposition
handoff. Preserve cumulative proof; do not reset it around a second-consumer
implementation.

## Locked decisions

- The second consumer must differ materially in domain types, outcomes,
  artifacts, policies, effects, storage, root map, timeout, JoinPolicy,
  resource settlement and composition shape. A renamed Megaplan fixture does
  not qualify.
- It imports multiple shared implementations from clean wheels with no Megaplan
  product code, copied pattern or product-specific introspection. Generic
  Arnold platform and reusable-component imports are expected and are not
  forbidden.
- Independence is proved by a content-addressed manifest and an independently
  executed source/dependency/schema/shape/lineage scan, not a self-description.
- `new_instance_compatible` and `resume_compatible` get independent content-
  addressed receipts. The former never implies the latter.
- Suspended runs use retained pinned artifacts, a declared compatible resume,
  an admitted migration/new run, or quarantine. Latest code is never an
  implicit resume rule.
- Different consumer values remain typed bindings; pressure from the second
  domain must not promote either product's prompts, models, policies, effects,
  storage or outcomes into platform defaults.
- S5 may revise or remove candidate abstractions, but still cannot publish
  stable registry status. S6 owns promotion after all amended proof is green.
- The unrelated consumer must originate its own domain completion obligations
  and composition. It must use the exact extracted kernel, authoritative
  decoder, distinct completion/enforcement registries and total mapping, and
  Custody bounded-query contract without copying Megaplan fixtures or
  completion implementations.
- It may supply different domain obligations, but it may not redefine
  candidate-first evaluation, executable obligation identity, evidence-window
  coordinates, proof modes, child-disposition algebra, waiver taint, verifier
  independence, quarantine policy, or reopen identity.

## Required work

1. Build a real non-Megaplan workflow using multiple S4 patterns in at least two
   shapes Megaplan does not use, with different nesting, retry, suspension,
   cancellation, effect/storage and resource behavior.
   Build and test it in an isolated lock where the Megaplan distribution is not
   installed.
2. Challenge the settled format with a package containing several separate
   one-workflow `.pype` files, canonical child imports, private-step isolation,
   shared `.py` steps, descriptor-owned optional default selection, explicit
   non-default selection, and aliases. Exercise a physical file/resource move,
   a logical rename with migration, package/version/dependency evolution, and
   checkout/wheel/cloud equivalence. Run monolith, needless-file-explosion,
   hidden-handler, private-import, `.py` durable-workflow,
   source/descriptor-mismatch, cycle, recursion, and collision negatives.
3. Bind distinct domain types/outcomes, artifacts, storage/effects, prompt/
   model/budget values, a different statically total root map, human-timeout
   disposition, outcome condition and JoinPolicy qualification/impossibility
   policy. Include parent cancellation during a live child/effect.
   Include absence/negative obligations, at least one human or effect boundary,
   waiver-taint propagation, dynamic child aggregation, and rework or analogous
   newly admitted work whose identities are instantiated at admission.
   Mutate every candidate disposition before obligation selection. Prove
   blocked and waived candidates fail without their typed proof, quarantine
   stays nonterminal without an admitted terminal policy, and unknown
   disposition/policy pairs reject.
   Prove presence separately from complete-capture absence: an absence claim
   stays unknown when the normative capture window is incomplete.
   Mutate the frozen child set, every child disposition, duplicate/missing
   child multiplicity, evidence reuse and double counting; require S2R's total
   mapping and transitive waiver taint to hold.
4. Exercise one LLM-backed shared component with different bindings and one
   checkpoint payload requiring an artifact reference. Preserve replay, cache,
   budget, retention and redaction semantics without platform defaults.
5. Substitute an independently implemented descriptor-compatible pattern for
   newly admitted instances and prove observational compatibility across ports,
   outcome conditions/emission, business/control results, lifecycle, decisions,
   effects, raw/normalized traces and terminals.
6. Prove resume compatibility separately. Include one admitted provenance-
   bearing migration and successful resume, one retained pinned-old resume, and
   one no-artifact/no-migration quarantine rather than latest-code fallback.
   Exercise old and current completion records through the authoritative
   decoder, including incompatible-version fail-closed behavior and an explicit
   admitted migration path.
   Independently mutate each evidence-window coordinate, including store
   incarnation and cursor bounds, and require reuse invalidation. Verifiers
   with the same producer identity or insufficient trust class cannot satisfy
   an independence obligation.
7. Run the ordinary suspended-run evolution matrix: body-only, topology/control,
   prompt, model/tool/policy, port/outcome/state/checkpoint schema, dependency,
   effect, generated manifest and producer registration. Include a v1 human
   gate suspended while v2 deploys and the promised old-wheel/prompt/tool
   retention window.
8. Mutate descriptor self-declarations and transitive resolved bindings to
   prove mechanically derived capability closure governs admission, migration,
   substitution and eventual publication. An independent verifier re-derives
   closure; per-capability inclusion/removal/rebind mutations must change the
   applicable family set and invalidate prior receipts.
9. Use product-neutral tooling to reconstruct both consumers' component,
   authority, parent/child, agent/LLM/tool/effect/resource and terminal histories
   without importing either product package.
10. Exercise identical five-mode/six-disposition behavior in both consumers:
   edited-step repeat, changed-code fork, non-authoritative comparison and a
   separately admitted pinned production run. Prove experiment namespaces and
   logs cannot collide with Megaplan, the second consumer's production history,
   or one another.
11. Record every abstraction narrowed, generalized, removed or retained. Amend
    the candidate contract/version and rerun all affected S1–S4 evidence rather
    than accepting compatibility shims that hide divergent semantics.
12. Produce `unrelated-consumer-independence-manifest` binding the isolated
    lock; zero Megaplan domain schema/type/outcome/root-adapter/policy/effect/
    storage/helper imports or copied definitions; a materially non-isomorphic
    outcome algebra with at least one domain outcome/condition absent from
    Megaplan; the two novel composition shapes; materially different root,
    timeout/join/resource/effect/storage bindings; independent implementation
    lineage; source tree; dependency graph; schema/vocabulary comparison;
    shape diff; commit; and lock. Generic lifecycle/business terms such as
    success, cancelled, timeout, or failed may overlap and do not count as
    independence evidence. Have a verifier outside the consumer/component
    producer path execute the scan.
13. Exercise one governed trace-field/volatile-allowlist amendment against
    pinned raw histories. Prove independent approval, affected-receipt
    invalidation, old/new replay, and rejection of retroactive result
    laundering.
14. Admit one reopened subject as a new admission referencing the immutable
    prior subject. Mutations that edit the prior binding, reuse its
    `(spec_hash, obligation_id)` execution identity as a new occurrence, or
    relabel prior evidence must fail.
15. Rerun the neutral-kernel import lint with Megaplan absent and prove the
    unrelated product adapter points inward to the neutral package. Product,
    CLI and platform disposition tables must be exact generated projections of
    the two canonical registries; local extensions or drift fail.

## Gates

### Semantic gate

- The unrelated consumer changes domain meaning and composition shape while the
  shared protocol remains product-neutral and sole source-authored routes remain
  visible in its `.pype` code.
- Its selected logical workflow and canonical import/lock graph are identical
  across checkout/wheel/cloud; declaration/resource ordering and pure physical
  moves are identity-inert, while logical/behavior/version changes take the
  declared compatibility/migrate/new-run/quarantine path.
- Independent substitution preserves the declared local contract; suspended
  instances never cross executable/state/effect versions without a separately
  admitted resume-compatible path or migration.
- Both products differ only in declared bindings and policies, not hidden runner,
  handler, scheduler, registry or projection control flow.
- The machine independence scan distinguishes permitted generic Arnold/
  reusable-component imports from forbidden Megaplan product coupling and
  proves the independent implementation has no fork/copy lineage.

### Proof gate

- Separate content-addressed `new_instance_compatible` and
  `resume_compatible` receipts exist and neither imports the other's verdict.
- The full change matrix proves pinned-old, compatible resume, explicit
  migration/new run and quarantine dispositions with artifact retention/GC
  boundaries. One real migration preserves provenance.
- Both consumers pass the same black-box static/lifecycle/isolation/recomposition/
  authority/effect/trace/mode suite and the same independently verified
  production-CAS profiles selected by actual capability closure.
- Root-result provenance, atomic condition disposition, human/join arbitration,
  resource settlement, unresolved-child facts and raw/partial-order truth
  survive the independent implementation and upgrade.
- Candidate-first evaluation, typed blocked/waived proof, nonterminal
  quarantine, presence versus complete-capture absence, the normative evidence
  window, verifier producer/trust independence, total child disposition,
  multiplicity/no-double-counting, waiver taint and reopen-as-new-admission all
  pass their negative mutations.
- No Megaplan import/copy exists; generic explanation works without product
  imports; the isolated lock succeeds with Megaplan absent; the independence
  manifest/scan is green; per-iteration agent/LLM/tool/effect/cost logs remain
  exact and queryable.

### Adoption gate

- Every candidate has an adversarial evidence-backed disposition: retained for
  S6 certification, revised with rerun evidence, removed, or explicitly still
  experimental. Consumer-specific leakage blocks S6.
- No candidate, descriptor, profile or package version is labeled stable merely
  because S5 passed.

## Artifacts and S6 handoff

Produce the unrelated consumer and clean-wheel isolated lock; executable
independence manifest and independent scan; novel-shape/binding fixtures;
independently originated implementation; separate compatibility and migration/
quarantine receipts; profile re-derivation mutations; governed allowlist
replay/invalidation evidence; cross-consumer explanation/mode/trace evidence;
updated contract/version and cumulative proof map; and a content-addressed
challenge report explaining every candidate decision. The handoff identifies
exactly which versions and capability profiles S6 may consider for stability.

## Do not close this sprint if

- the second consumer is Megaplan-shaped, imports/copies Megaplan, or avoids the
  unfamiliar policies and shapes that challenge the abstraction;
- the independence manifest/scan is missing, producer-only-verified, forbids
  legitimate generic platform imports, or cannot build with Megaplan absent;
- new-instance evidence is reused as resume evidence, or latest code resumes a
  pinned suspended occurrence;
- a compatibility shim hides different product control flow or evidence is
  self-certified/normalized before raw conservation checks;
- experimental histories/authority/effects can collide or be promoted; or
- any unresolved second-consumer leakage is deferred while claiming the
  candidate ready for stable publication.

## Non-goals

- Stable publication, deprecation/withdrawal operations or marketplace work.
- Rebuilding M11/Native authority substrate, forcing the consumer into
  Megaplan outcomes, or standardizing its product values.
- Adding unsupported open streams or arbitrary durable Python to make the
  consumer fit.
