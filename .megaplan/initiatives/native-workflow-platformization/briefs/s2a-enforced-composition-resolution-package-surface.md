# S2A — Enforced composition, resolution, and runtime package substrate

## Objective

Promote the accepted Native/Native-Parity runtime and adapters **in place** into
one product-neutral package, compiler, lifecycle, admission, composition,
observation, and local-test surface. Remove product coupling without replacing
the canonical implementations or their proof lineage. Make invalid
compositions fail before authority and make valid compositions retain
identical semantics across checkout, clean wheel, cloud and the faithful local
harness. This is the reusable runtime hinge; it remains experimental.

## Normative contract and inputs

[`../PLATFORM_CONTRACT.md`](../PLATFORM_CONTRACT.md) is normative. This brief
assigns S2A work and proof; it cannot weaken that contract. Consume the exact
Native Parity completion/handoff receipts plus S1's content-addressed candidate
standard, extraction inventory, executable models, negative/DX corpora,
diagnostic catalog, conformance/traceability skeletons and open-row proof map.

The Native Parity handoff remains the source for accepted RA/Custody/WBC,
effects, real production CAS, restore, controlled-producer and trace behavior.
S2A promotes those exact canonical contracts and adapters; it does not emulate,
fork, shadow, wrap-and-replace, or rebuild missing substrate. A blocked in-place
promotion is repaired at the canonical owner and blocks S2A.

The intake also pins the Native C1/C2 manifests, S2R GO-0 enablement receipt,
current divergence-ledger hash, and the Custody bounded-projection/57k receipt.
S2A extracts the exact authoritative completion path in place:
`CompletionSpec -> CompletionBinding -> CompletionVerdict -> existing
acceptance transaction`. It cannot re-enable the kernel or create another
writer, decoder, ledger, evidence store, or incident projection.

## Receipt-bound promotion

Implementation and proof may land in the canonical modules, but the candidate
runtime version remains unselected for new admitted product work through
merge. Existing occurrences remain pinned to their accepted version. The S2A
stage gate then runs against merge HEAD. Only an explicit registry/admission-
binding promotion operation that validates and consumes that exact accepted
S2A receipt may select the new product-neutral version for new admitted work.
The operation is atomic, records the old/new identities and lock, and never
uses a milestone state, filename, status label, or copied verdict as authority.
It runs only as the chain's declared typed S2A transition. The chain records
its output receipt and reruns the S2A post-transition conformance gate against
the selected state; S2A cannot complete if that verifier fails.

## Locked decisions

- One implementation of the S1 contract serves all products and all five
  execution modes. The local harness substitutes adapters and time, not
  lifecycle/admission/event semantics.
- The accepted implementation is promoted in place. Existing identities,
  compatibility readers, histories, restoration semantics, adapter provenance,
  and proof lineage remain attached to the canonical surface; no
  Platformization-local runtime fork or facade may become the new authority.
- Validation and mechanically derived capability closure run before lowering
  and authority acquisition. Source-authored topology cannot be altered by a
  generated manifest, registry, handler or adapter.
- Preview/sandbox/comparison identities, histories, authority, caches,
  checkpoints and effect/idempotency domains are disjoint from production.
  Store/capability access follows the sole
  `docs/arnold/workflow-execution-mode-dispositions.yaml` registry, including
  when logical classes
  share a physical backend.
- Common lifecycle, total root hosting, atomic outcome conditions, human CAS,
  durable loops/joins/cancellation/resources, typed exits/reconfiguration and
  declared agentic boundaries are implemented exactly once.
- Every authority-bearing arbitration/consumption site uses certified
  production-store/service linearizable conditional mutation. The fake CAS is
  useful local infrastructure but never proof of production authority.
- Qualified component identity and the complete content-addressed transitive
  lock bind admission and checkpoints. Mixed or unavailable versions fail
  closed before product work.
- Raw event identity and multiplicity are checked before versioned partial-
  order normalization. Unknown fields fail; normalization never deduplicates or
  erases causality/arbitration.
- Package surfaces and registry status remain experimental. S2A does not publish
  reusable product patterns as stable.
- Immutable completion binding, exact evidence scope, proof modes, aggregation
  signatures, verifier independence, waiver taint, compatibility checks,
  authoritative decoding, inspection, and the atomic acceptance adapter move
  together without semantic change. Concrete durable-primitive aggregation
  instances remain the exact Native S2R implementation.
- Completion candidate evaluation remains candidate-first. Blocked and waived
  candidates require their typed proofs; quarantine remains nonterminal unless
  its exact admitted terminal policy applies. Executable obligation identity is
  `(spec_hash, obligation_id)` with stable semantic IDs, and the evidence window
  is the exact inherited tuple rather than a Platform-selected query range.
- Presence and complete-capture absence verifiers remain distinct. Verifier
  independence binds producer identity and trust class. Frozen child sets,
  total child-disposition mapping, multiplicity/no-double-counting and
  transitive waiver taint remain the exact S2R primitive instances.
- Completion candidate outcomes and enforcement dispositions remain distinct
  versioned registries connected only by their generated total version-bound
  mapping. Unknown pairs reject before admission.

## Gates

### Semantic gate

- Root, nested, sequential, loop and fanout components use one lifecycle and
  source-authored route set. All invalid descriptors, compositions, cycles,
  maps, joins, conditions, namespaces, locks, route data, effects/state and
  mode transitions fail before authority or effect intent.
- Runtime interfaces accept only one already-validated canonical workflow
  graph and reject missing/ambiguous identity, import/version collisions,
  cycles/recursion, hidden topology, and effectful helpers before authority.
  S2B owns file-level exact-one enforcement and authored-span rewrites.
- One descriptor-selected logical workflow is frozen across manifest, lock,
  admission, and checkpoint. S2B owns CLI/API selection and source/descriptor/
  installed-resource correspondence.
- Allowed pure helpers preserve their transitive dependencies/call sites,
  evaluate deterministically, participate in executable digest drift, and
  cannot hide forbidden I/O through another helper.
- Resume/replay/retry/rework/fork, same child/new generation, business/control
  result, and experimental/production identity are observably distinct.
- Reopen is a new admission that references the immutable prior subject and
  binding. It never mutates, reuses, or rebinds the prior admission.
- The local kit and installed runtime consume the same compiler, validators,
  lifecycle, events and recorded boundary outcomes.

### Proof gate

- Run lifecycle, crash and race fixtures across admission, body, checkpoint,
  effect, condition/local/root terminal, suspension/resume, compensation,
  parent-loop edges, human answers/timeouts/cancel, joins, cancellation/Custody,
  resource settlement and late completion.
- Run raw trace duplicate/drop/invert/unknown-field/false-order/route-smuggling
  mutations, governed allowlist amendment/replay/invalidation, source-manifest
  topology mutations, independent capability inclusion/removal/rebind, and
  under-declaration at admission/rebind/migration/lock changes.
- Independent production clients prove exactly one accepted mutation for every
  authority-bearing adapter; receipts bind store/service, adapter, key schema,
  consistency/deployment mode and proof-registry incarnation/high-water.
- Checkout, wheel, cloud and local/installed fixtures have identical locked
  graph and equivalent normalized lifecycle/admission traces for identical
  recorded boundary outcomes.
- Inherited/S1 diagnostics, ten-task author simulation, repeatability and
  compile/local p50/p95 thresholds pass in the pinned environment.
- Paired mode fixtures allow edited-code repeat/fork with fresh identity and
  reject silent resume, production-effect/authority/key/cache/checkpoint reuse,
  opaque fallback, evidence promotion and preview/comparison conformance credit.

### Adoption gate

- The package is importable and usable only as an experimental candidate
  surface with documentation sufficient for S2B core integration and S3
  tooling; S4 extraction consumes their completed handoffs.
- No product semantics or Megaplan defaults enter shared code. No registry item
  becomes stable, and the fake/local harness is not represented as production
  conformance evidence.
- The canonical implementation has one promoted-in-place lineage; scans and
  source/proof maps show no duplicate Platformization runtime, shadow adapter,
  parallel store, or compatibility facade.
- The canonical selection changes only through the receipt-consuming promotion
  operation after the S2A stage receipt binds merge HEAD.

## Artifacts and S2B handoff

Produce the experimental runtime package and lock interfaces; validators,
lifecycle/composition/admission engines; faithful local kit;
manifest/registry/effect/trace machinery; updated conformance and traceability
corpora; real-store CAS and clean-install/cloud receipts; DX measurements; and a
content-addressed S2A proof-map handoff naming the exact runtime interfaces
S2B must consume and every still-red S2B authoring-core, S3 DX/tooling, or later
profile.

## Do not close this sprint if

- any invalid composition can acquire authority or reach an effect body;
- any accepted runtime/adapter is forked, shadowed, wrapped-and-replaced, or
  rebuilt instead of promoted in place;
- the candidate can become selectable for new admitted work before the exact
  accepted post-merge S2A receipt is consumed;
- a generated artifact, registry, scheduler, projection or local runner can
  add/erase product control flow;
- fake CAS, stitched traces, self-verification, normalized-only evidence or a
  hash without semantic consumption is accepted as proof;
- experimental histories can collide with or be relabeled as production;
- source, wheel, cloud or local harness resolve different behavior; or
- S2B would need to invent lifecycle, composition, admission, durable identity,
  or authority rules.

## Non-goals

- Extracting Megaplan product patterns or building the second consumer.
- Publishing stable APIs, registry entries or compatibility promises.
- Repairing M11/Native substrate, generalizing product semantics, supporting
  arbitrary durable Python/open streams, or freezing internal compiler APIs.
