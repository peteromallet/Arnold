# North Star — Critique Ledger durable completion

The fresh Critique Ledger v3 epic completes through ordinary, owner-authorized
Megaplan execution; its product is accepted, merged, released, deployed and
proven in production; poisoned v2 remains immutable and incapable of effects;
and this incident becomes a permanent fail-closed regression gate.

Success is evidence, not status prose:

- each model attempt carries exact authenticated raw-byte custody;
- failed, unknown or unverifiable work never projects as clean success;
- every external effect has one WBC occurrence and exact replay semantics;
- one eligible failure creates at most one repair occurrence and one genuine
  decision notification;
- v3 advances from authoritative predecessor completion manifests;
- product deployment is content-addressed, independently verified and safely
  reversible;
- 24-hour, 72-hour and 7-day observations show no old writer, duplicate effect,
  stalled authority, projection disagreement or reserve breach.

No milestone may weaken owner separation, synthesize authority from caller
labels, edit generated state to manufacture progress, or use the retired cloud,
tmux, marker-edit, watchdog-relaunch or raw chain-start paths.

## Canonical substrate and minimality

This epic is a Critique Ledger adopter of the existing
[Custody Control Plane](../custody-control-plane/NORTHSTAR.md), not a second
control plane. Run Authority owns grants/fences/claims/decisions; WBC owns
attempt/effect evidence, provenance and receipts; Custody owns occurrence
identity, leases/epochs and recovery/reconciliation; projections and observers
are read-only. Critique owns critique semantics and product workflow only.

No milestone may introduce a competing ledger, event bus, snapshot authority,
fixer framework, daemon, or persistence substrate without an explicit reviewed
architecture exception. Every new writer or reader must name its owner and
retirement/expiry path, and every sprint must prove one thin end-to-end failure
slice through the existing WBC + Run Authority + Custody boundaries before
expanding its inventory. The bounded T6.2/current relaunch remains independent
of later platform-wide hardening.

The detailed acceptance and stop conditions are the committed
[`architecture-fit-and-minimality-gate-20260804.md`](evidence/architecture-fit-and-minimality-gate-20260804.md).

The Stage-A handoff proves only a bounded finalize transition. Before ordinary
execution/publication authority expands, this epic first completes the deferred
platform-wide owner/storage/recovery and admission/model/effect release work.
